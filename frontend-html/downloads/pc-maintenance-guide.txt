BITS AND BYTES - PC MAINTENANCE GUIDE

MONTHLY
- Check that vents are clear and fans spin normally.
- Review free storage space and remove unneeded temporary files.

EVERY THREE TO SIX MONTHS
- Shut down and unplug the computer before cleaning.
- Remove dust from filters and accessible vents.
- Check cables for strain, damage, or loose connections.
- Install stable driver and firmware updates from trusted sources.

BEFORE UPGRADING
- Confirm motherboard, case, power, and connector compatibility.
- Back up important files.
- Follow antistatic handling practices.

This sample manual is intended as a replaceable download for the storefront project.
