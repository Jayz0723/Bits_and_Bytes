BITS AND BYTES - DESKTOP SETUP CHECKLIST

1. Place the desktop on a stable surface with clear airflow around every vent.
2. Connect the monitor to the graphics card when the PC includes a dedicated GPU.
3. Connect the keyboard, mouse, network cable, and audio devices.
4. Connect the power cable through a quality surge protector or UPS.
5. Complete Windows setup and install current operating-system updates.
6. Install current graphics, chipset, network, and peripheral drivers.
7. Confirm the monitor is using its intended resolution and refresh rate.
8. Keep product packaging and warranty information for future support.

This sample manual is intended as a replaceable download for the storefront project.
