BITS AND BYTES - LAPTOP CARE MANUAL

1. Use the laptop on a firm surface so its cooling vents remain open.
2. Use the supplied charger or a compatible charger with the correct rating.
3. Keep liquids away from the keyboard and ports.
4. Install operating-system, firmware, and driver updates from trusted sources.
5. Transport the laptop in a padded sleeve and avoid pressure on the display.
6. Back up school, work, and personal files regularly.
7. Stop using the laptop if the battery swells or the chassis separates.
8. Clean vents with the device powered off and disconnected from power.

This sample manual is intended as a replaceable download for the storefront project.
