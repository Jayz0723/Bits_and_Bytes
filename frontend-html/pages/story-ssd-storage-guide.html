Choose the right SSD for your next upgrade

Capacity is only the beginning: compare interface, physical size and slot support.

By Bits & Bytes editorial · 4 min read

In this article

Match the connection Plan for your files Back up before changing drives
01

Match the connection

SATA and NVMe drives use different interfaces. An M.2-shaped slot alone does not guarantee NVMe support; read the device or motherboard documentation for the supported interface and length.

02

Plan for your files

Estimate space for the operating system, applications, games and projects, then leave room for growth. Compare warranty and endurance information for the exact drive capacity.

03

Back up before changing drives

Keep an independent backup before cloning or replacing a drive. Decide whether to migrate your system or perform a fresh installation, and retain the old drive until your files are verified.
