Privacy

How the Bits and Bytes website handles your information and shopping preferences.

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Shopping information on your device

Your cart, wishlist, language, currency, music preference, and optionally remembered email are saved in your browser's local storage. They remain there until you change them or clear this website's stored data.

Account information

When the store runs through its local database server, registration stores your name, email, randomly generated salt, and password hash in SQLite on the server computer. Your password is not stored as plain text. An HttpOnly session cookie keeps you signed in for up to seven days. Addresses are associated with your account and can be accessed from other browsers connected to the same server after signing in. Use a password unique to this store and sign out on shared devices. Opening the HTML files directly uses the older browser-only account storage instead; those accounts are not automatically imported into the database.

Support messages and photos

The contact form prepares a message in the page for you to review; it does not send it automatically. Selected photos are previewed locally and are not uploaded by the form. The store chat is an AI assistant, not a live support agent. When you send a chat message, the server sends that message and a limited recent conversation history to OpenAI to generate a reply. Chat history stays in page memory and is cleared on reload; it is not saved in the store database. Requests use OpenAI's store:false option, but OpenAI's applicable service retention and privacy terms still apply. The store does not automatically include account details, addresses, passwords or orders in AI requests. Do not type sensitive information into chat. Information you choose to send through an external social platform is handled by that platform.

Checkout details

Completing sample checkout saves an order reference, item selections, recipient name, contact details, delivery address, amount, payment-method label, sample payment reference, and sample delivery events in this browser. These records are linked to the local signed-in account and remain until this website's site data is cleared. Card numbers, card security codes, wallet PINs, and OTPs are not saved in the order record. Do not enter real card credentials into the sample card form.

My account displays your local purchase history, recent delivery addresses, and favorites. Guest favorites move into the account when you sign in. Return/refund requests save an order reference, reason, description, amount, status, and timestamp on this device only. They are not automatically sent to the store and do not issue a refund. Do not include payment passwords or PINs in a request.

Your address book stores recipient names, phone numbers, delivery addresses, optional instructions, and a default-address choice. With the local server, these are stored in SQLite under your account, not only in browser storage. You can edit or remove saved addresses in My account. Signing out or clearing browser data does not erase database addresses. In direct-file mode they remain browser-only. Past orders retain their original delivery details. Orders, favorites and sample tracking still stay in the browser; they are not synchronized by the account database.

Sample verification does not contact GCash, a bank, or a courier. The owner-provided GCash QR is a public image on this website and is separate from the non-payable sample QR. Do not send money to test the site.

External services

Some pages request fonts from Google Fonts. Social links and manufacturer links open third-party websites with their own privacy practices. When the store is accessed through a hosting service, that service may receive request information such as your IP address and browser details. The website's own code does not include advertising or analytics scripts.

The delivery form includes a Google Maps preview. Loading or interacting with it connects your browser to Google, which may receive your IP address, browser details, website origin, selected general delivery area and map interactions. The store sends only your selected city, province or region and country to the map, not your name, phone number or typed street address. Store pickup shows the fictional collection-point coordinates. The embedded map does not fill your shipping address automatically; your delivery details are taken from the form. Google's privacy policy applies to its map service.

Managing your information

Sign out from the account page to end the current sign-in. To remove the local account, saved orders, delivery details, cart, wishlist, and preferences, clear this website's site data in your browser settings. Clearing that data removes local accounts and saved shopping information; signing out alone does not. Other people with access to your browser profile may be able to inspect its stored data. For questions about information you have shared through customer support, contact us using the contact page.
