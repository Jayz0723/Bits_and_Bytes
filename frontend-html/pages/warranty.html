Warranty Information

Keep your receipt and product packaging. The exact warranty period and coverage depend on the product and its manufacturer.

What is normally covered

Eligible manufacturing defects and hardware failures arising during normal use may be covered within the warranty period shown on the receipt or product listing.

What is not normally covered

  • Accidental, liquid, impact, or cosmetic damage
  • Damage caused by misuse, improper installation, or unauthorized repair
  • Normal wear, consumable parts, or lost accessories
  • Removed, altered, or unreadable serial numbers

Before requesting service

Back up important data, remove account passwords where possible, and prepare your proof of purchase, serial number, and a clear description of the issue.

Warranty process

Contact support for an initial assessment. Approved items may require inspection before repair, replacement, store credit, or manufacturer service is confirmed.

Start a warranty request

Send the product name, order reference, purchase date, and details of the problem to our support team.
