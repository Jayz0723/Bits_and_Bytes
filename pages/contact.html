A little clarity

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about products, custom PC builds, delivery, and payment.

How do I know if a PC part is compatible with my build?

Open Build Your Own PC and select your parts. The builder checks important details such as CPU socket, RAM type, case size, and estimated power needs.

Can I see product specifications and stock before adding an item?

Yes. Select Details on any product card to see specifications, price, availability, delivery information, and payment options.

What payment methods can I choose at checkout?

You can review Cash on Delivery, card, GCash, and Maya preferences at checkout. Online payment processing is not connected; no payment is taken on this website.

How does delivery address selection work?

In the cart checkout, choose a region or province first, then a city or municipality, followed by a barangay. The postal code is filled in when a city is selected.

Can I save an item and decide later?

Yes. Use the heart button on a product card to save it to your wishlist. Your saved items remain available while you browse the store.
