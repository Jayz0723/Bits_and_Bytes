Your information

Privacy

How the Bits and Bytes website handles your information and shopping preferences.

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Shopping information on your device

Your cart, wishlist, language, currency, music preference, and optionally remembered email are saved in your browser's local storage. They remain there until you change them or clear this website's stored data.

Account information

Registration saves your name, email, a randomly generated salt, and a password hash in this browser. Your password is not saved as plain text. The signed-in email is kept in session storage. Accounts do not synchronize between browsers or devices. Use a password unique to this store and avoid registering on shared devices.

Support messages and photos

The contact form prepares a message in the page for you to review; it does not send it automatically. Selected photos are previewed locally and are not uploaded by the form. The store chat provides automated guidance within the page, not a live conversation with a support agent. Information you choose to send through an external social platform is handled by that platform.

Checkout details

Contact, delivery, and payment fields are used for an on-page order review. The current checkout does not submit an order or process a payment. Do not enter real card numbers or other sensitive payment credentials.

External services

Some pages request fonts from Google Fonts. Social links and manufacturer links open third-party websites with their own privacy practices. When the store is accessed through a hosting service, that service may receive request information such as your IP address and browser details. The website's own code does not include advertising or analytics scripts.

Managing your information

Sign out from the account page to end the current sign-in. To remove the local account, saved cart, wishlist, and preferences, clear this website's site data in your browser settings. Clearing that data removes local accounts and saved shopping information; signing out alone does not. For questions about information you have shared through customer support, contact us using the contact page.
